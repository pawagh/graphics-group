The system enabled visualization of large 3D models on a multi-screen 2D display on a single wall for telepresence applications. We experimented with large datasets, such as reconstructed models of the Roman Colosseum, and dense datasets, such as highly detailed models of an indoor room, and found the visualization to be very convincing for a single user. We experimented with a number of ways to achieve a satisfactory visual experience for multiple users, but none of our methods provided the realism and the experience required of modern-day telepresence systems.
